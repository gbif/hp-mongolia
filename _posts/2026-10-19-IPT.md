# GBIF Mongolia Launches National IPT for Biodiversity Data Publishing

**GBIF Mongolia** has launched its national **Integrated Publishing Toolkit (IPT)**, providing a dedicated platform for Mongolian institutions and researchers to publish and share biodiversity data through the global GBIF network.

The new platform, available at [ipt.gbif.mn](https://ipt.gbif.mn/), represents an important step toward strengthening biodiversity data mobilization and making information about Mongolia’s biodiversity more accessible to researchers and other users worldwide.

## What is the GBIF IPT?

The **Integrated Publishing Toolkit (IPT)** is an open-source tool developed by GBIF to help institutions publish biodiversity datasets in a standardized format and make them accessible through the GBIF network.

Through the IPT, data providers can prepare and publish different types of biodiversity information, including species occurrence records, taxonomic information, and other biodiversity-related datasets.

Once published and shared through GBIF, these data can be discovered and accessed by researchers and other users around the world.

## A New Platform for Mongolian Biodiversity Data

The launch of the national IPT provides a dedicated space for biodiversity data providers in Mongolia to manage and publish their datasets.

The platform will support collaboration between researchers, universities, museums, research institutions, and other organizations holding biodiversity information in Mongolia.

By encouraging institutions to publish their data in a standardized and accessible way, GBIF Mongolia aims to help bring together biodiversity information that is currently distributed across different collections, research projects, and institutions.

## Connecting Mongolia's Data to the World

Biodiversity data collected in Mongolia are valuable not only for research within the country but also for understanding species and ecosystems at regional and global scales.

Through the GBIF network, published datasets can contribute to a growing international resource of openly accessible biodiversity information. This can support research in areas such as biodiversity monitoring, species distribution, conservation, environmental assessment, and ecological research.

The national IPT therefore provides an important link between **Mongolian biodiversity data and the global scientific community**.

## Supporting Open Biodiversity Data

The development of the GBIF Mongolia IPT is part of ongoing efforts to strengthen biodiversity data infrastructure in Mongolia and encourage greater participation in international biodiversity data sharing.

GBIF Mongolia welcomes institutions and researchers interested in publishing biodiversity data and contributing to the growing collection of openly accessible information about Mongolia’s biodiversity.

Visit the [GBIF Mongolia IPT](https://ipt.gbif.mn/) to explore the platform and learn more about biodiversity data publishing.
