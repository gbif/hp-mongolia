# GBIF Mongolia launches national IPT platform for publishing biodiversity data

**GBIF Mongolia** has launched a national **Integrated Publishing Toolkit (IPT)** platform to support the preparation and publication of Mongolia's biodiversity data in accordance with international standards.

The platform enables organizations and researchers in Mongolia to publish their biodiversity data through the global GBIF network, making it accessible to the international research community and other users.

The newly launched [GBIF Mongolia IPT platform](https://ipt.gbif.mn/) represents an important step toward strengthening Mongolia's national infrastructure for sharing and accessing biodiversity data.

## What is IPT?

**Integrated Publishing Toolkit (IPT)** is open-source software developed by GBIF for publishing biodiversity data.

IPT allows data-publishing organizations and researchers to organize their datasets, prepare them according to relevant standards, and publish them together with their metadata.

The platform can be used to publish a range of biodiversity datasets, including:

- Species occurrence records
- Observation records
- Museum and herbarium collection data
- Taxonomic data
- Other biodiversity datasets

## Mongolia's National IPT Platform

The national IPT platform provides a centralized environment where biodiversity data from research institutions, universities, museums, and other data-holding organizations in Mongolia can be prepared and published.

Through the platform, data publishers can:

- Prepare datasets according to GBIF standards;
- Manage datasets and their metadata;
- Publish datasets through the GBIF network;
- Update and maintain published datasets.

This provides a more coordinated approach to publishing biodiversity data that may previously have been maintained separately across different institutions, research projects, and collections.

## Connecting Mongolia's Data to Global Research

Mongolia's vast territory, diverse ecosystems, and unique species make its biodiversity data valuable not only for national research but also for regional and global studies.

Once published through the GBIF network, these datasets can be openly accessed and used for a wide range of applications, including:

- Biodiversity monitoring;
- Species distribution and status assessments;
- Conservation planning;
- Environmental impact assessments;
- Ecological and climate-related research.

Making these datasets discoverable through GBIF increases opportunities for researchers to incorporate observations and records from Mongolia into broader biodiversity analyses and research.

## Supporting Open Data and Collaboration

The launch of the national IPT platform is part of **GBIF Mongolia's** efforts to strengthen Mongolia's biodiversity data infrastructure, promote open data sharing, and expand Mongolia's participation in the international **Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF)** network.

**GBIF Mongolia** invites organizations and researchers holding biodiversity datasets to join this initiative and contribute to the development of an open and accessible national biodiversity data resource.

Visit the [GBIF Mongolia IPT platform](https://ipt.gbif.mn/) to learn more about the platform and opportunities for publishing biodiversity data.
